# Research and product boundaries

## What the prototype demonstrates

EPFO Resolve uses a single, synthetic Ananya Rao case to demonstrate a safe product pattern: identify a contradictory historical EPS entry, collect supporting fictional evidence, request a correction, preserve the audit trail through a missed *proposed* response target, and explain a balance-preserving reclassification before the simulated transfer completes.

## Sources used for context

- [EPFO FAQ](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_en/FAQ.php) is linked beside the rule explanation. It is contextual source material, not a live eligibility lookup.
- [EPFO Citizen Charter](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/MiscPDFs/CitizenCharter.pdf) informs the surrounding Form 13 and grievance context.

The experience does not claim to interpret a real account or make a legal decision. The invented seven-business-day employer response target is visibly labelled as a product simulation; it is not represented as an EPFO service level or statutory deadline.

## Decisions deliberately kept deterministic

- Case eligibility facts and state transitions
- Required evidence and the balance-conservation calculation
- Escalation sequencing and the final synthetic transfer outcome

AI can only restate whitelisted fictional facts, draft a fictional correction note, or translate context. Any unavailable AI call returns curated fallback text and says so.
